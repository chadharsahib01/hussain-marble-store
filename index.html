About Hussain Marble Factory

Founded in 2010 in the heart of Mor Khunda, Hussain Marble Factory has grown into the leading natural stone and construction material supplier in the Nankana Sahib District. We specialize in providing a complete, all-in-one Floor-to-Ceiling solution for homeowners, builders, and contractors, ensuring unmatched quality, durability, and transparent digitized billing.

From precision marble cutting and crafting our signature 4x4 stone pools to producing fresh A-grade gypsum ceiling tiles onsite, our dedicated team handles every project with expertise. We pride ourselves on factory-direct pricing with no middlemen, and reliable local logistics serving communities like Bucheki, Manawala, Surab Kalan, and Surab Khurd.
